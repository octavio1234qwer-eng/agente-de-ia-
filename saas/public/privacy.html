Who we are

[Company legal name], located at [full address], is responsible for processing your personal data. Privacy contact: [privacy email].

Data we collect

  • Identity and contact: name, email and, optionally, your business name and country.
  • Credentials: your password is stored hashed (scrypt); we cannot read it.
  • Content: the text you enter in the tools and the generated results.
  • Usage and security: sign-in dates, IP address and browser of each session, and credit usage records.
  • Payments: your card details are processed directly by Stripe; we only receive the status of your subscription and payments.

Please do not enter sensitive personal data about yourself or others in the tools.

Why we use it

To create and manage your account, generate the results you request, bill your subscription, apply usage limits, prevent fraud and abuse, and answer support and billing requests. With your consent, to send you product news; you can opt out at any time.

Processors and international transfers

We share data with providers that process it on our behalf: hosting and database ([provider]), payments (Stripe), AI model provider ([OpenAI or Anthropic]), which receives the text you send to each tool, and email ([provider]). Some are located outside your country. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and request a copy of it. From your account you can download your data (My account → Download my data), edit your profile and delete your account with all your history. For any other request, email [privacy email]. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, California under the CCPA, or Mexico), you may have additional rights and may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Retention

Your history content is kept according to your plan (7 days on Free, 1 year on Pro, 2 years on Business) or until you delete it. Payment records are kept as long as tax laws require.

Cookies

We use a single technical cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Changes
